# Step 3: Clean Data

It’s time to address the issues found during data assessment and **clean your data** to ensure it is ready for analysis.

In this step, you’ll focus on cleaning the data to resolve the quality and tidiness issues identified in the assessment phase. Be sure to include clear justifications for each cleaning decision you make, and, **after each cleaning step, use either a visual or programmatic method to validate that the issue was successfully resolved**.

**Note**: At this stage, you are also expected to remove unnecessary variables for your analysis and to combine your datasets into a single, cohesive dataset. Depending on your data structure, you may choose to perform variable removal and dataset merging either before or after addressing the data quality and tidiness issues. Your final, combined dataset must have at least four variables after completing this step.

## Task List

Before proceeding, make sure you’ve completed the following:

- [ ] **Clean All Identified Issues**: Endure that all data quality and tidiness issues identified during the assessment phase are addressed. This includes handling missing values, correcting invalid entries, and restructuring data to align with tidiness rules.
- [ ] **Validate Each Cleaning Step**: After each cleaning action, use either a visual or programmatic method (e.g., plotting, `.isnull()`, `.info()`) to confirm that the cleaning was successful and that the data is now free from the identified issue.
- [ ] **Provide Justifications**: For each cleaning method you apply, briefly explain why you chose this approach and why it is appropriate for addressing the specific issue.
- [ ] **Add Code Comments**: Include comments in your code to ensure readability and clarify the purpose of each cleaning step.
- [ ] **Remove Unnecessary Variables**: Identify and remove any variables that are irrelevant to your analysis. This process can also be referred to as a basic form of dimensionality reduction, as it simplifies your dataset to include only meaningful data.
- [ ] **Combine Datasets**: Merge your datasets in a way that aligns with the analytical goals. Ensure that your final combined dataset includes at least four variables after cleaning and variable elimination.
