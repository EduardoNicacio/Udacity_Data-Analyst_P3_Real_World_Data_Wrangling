## 1.2. Gather at least two datasets using two different data-gathering methods

In this part of the project, you will gather **two related datasets using two different data-gathering methods**. The related datasets should have a common theme or a shared variable, which will allow you to combine them logically for analysis.

- **Finance**: Gather stock price data from one source (e.g., Yahoo Finance) and pair it with economic indicators like GDP or inflation rates from another source (e.g., the World Bank). This combination could allow analysis of how economic conditions influence stock prices.
- **Healthcare**: Combine patient demographic data from one source with medication or treatment data from another, ensuring the data is anonymized. This combination could support analysis of how demographics influence treatment outcomes.
- **Sports**: Use player performance statistics from one dataset and team financial investment data from another. This would allow an analysis of the relationship between financial investment and player performance.
- **Retail**: Gather online sales data through an open-source e-commerce dataset, and pair it with social media sentiment data related to products sold. This combination enables analysis of how social media trends impact sales.
- **Environmental Studies**: Collect weather data from one source and pollution metrics from another to examine the relationship between weather conditions and pollution levels.
- **Education**: Combine data on student performance (e.g., test scores or graduation rates) from one source with school funding data or demographic information from another source. This allows for an analysis of how socioeconomic factors or funding disparities impact student outcomes.
- **Transportation**: Collect public transportation usage statistics (e.g., ridership data by station) from one source and traffic or air quality data from another. Analyzing these datasets together could reveal correlations between public transport usage and environmental or traffic conditions.

Choose datasets that contain quality and tidiness issues (e.g., missing values, inconsistencies, or messy structures) to ensure you can demonstrate your skills in data cleaning and wrangling.

**Note**: Before investing significant time in cleaning and EDA, make sure the datasets have quality and tidiness issues that meet the project’s assessment requirements.
